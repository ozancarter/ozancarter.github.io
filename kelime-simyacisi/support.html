How to play

Kelime Simyacısı is a Turkish word game. You get two elements (for example 🔥 Ateş + 💨 Hava) and guess the word their combination makes. After each guess the letters change colour: 🟩 green is the right letter in the right place, 🟨 yellow is in the word but elsewhere, grey is not in the word. You have 5 guesses per level; if you get stuck, use 💡 Harf (letter) or 📖 İpucu (hint).

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your device. There are no accounts or cloud sync, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts the game over.

Why do I see ads?
Ads keep the game free and only appear when you choose them: when you tap “Reklam İzle” (Watch Ad) for extra guesses, a hint or crystals, or when you give up to see the answer.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also use Ayarlar → Reklam Tercihlerim inside the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
