Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short: Kelime Simyacısı has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other personal information. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob uses to show ads, and you control it with the options below.

Who we are

Kelime Simyacısı (“the app”) is made by KryoTale Studio (“we”). For any question about this policy, write to KryoTaleStudio@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

To remember where you left off, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • your level, the elements you have discovered (your collection) and your crystal count,
  • your daily quest and streak,
  • your settings (music, sound effects, vibration),
  • counters that enforce the daily limit on ad rewards.

This data is never sent to us. We do not run servers and we do not use analytics tools. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Kelime Simyacısı is free. Ads appear only when you choose them: when you tap “Watch Ad” to get extra guesses, a hint or crystals, or when you give up to see the answer. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited). To serve and measure ads, cap how often they appear and prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version,
  • ad interaction data such as which ads were shown or tapped,
  • diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

Google collects and processes this data under its own policies; it does not reach us in a form that identifies you. Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • iPhone: before your advertising identifier is used, the app asks for permission with Apple’s tracking prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, it is not shared. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google’s consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer at any time in the app under Ayarlar → Reklam Tercihlerim (Settings → Ad Preferences).
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location. Music, sound and vibration are produced on your device and can be turned off in the app’s settings.

Children

Kelime Simyacısı is a word game for players of all ages and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences and parental guidance.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. To exercise your rights under the GDPR (EEA / UK) or Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), or to raise a concern, write to the address above. For data collected by Google you can also use the tools on Google’s privacy page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
