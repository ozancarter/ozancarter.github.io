How to play

Kelime İstasyonu is a Turkish word game. There are 3 trains at the top, each with 4 empty wagons. Drag a card from the depot, or tap a card and then a train. When the 4 words of one group are on the same train, the category is revealed and the train departs (for example KEMAN, FLÜT, DAVUL, PİYANO → 🎻 Musical Instruments). Move top cards to the platform to reach the ones below; the platform holds only 3 cards. Some words fit two groups: put one on the wrong train and you lose a heart.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your device. There are no accounts or cloud sync, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts the game over.

How do I earn diamonds?
By finishing stops. At the end of a stop you can watch an optional ad to double the reward. Diamonds cannot be bought with real money; they only unlock designs in the shop.

Why do I see ads?
Ads keep the game free. There are no ads in the first 7 stops; after that, short ads appear between some stops.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
On iPhone go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android use Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve reklamlar in the app. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland there is also Ayarlar → Reklam tercihlerim. See the Privacy Policy.
