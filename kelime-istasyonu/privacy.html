Kelime İstasyonu Türkçe

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

In short: Kelime İstasyonu has no accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail or any other personal information. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob uses to show ads, and you control it with the options below.

Who we are

Kelime İstasyonu (“the app”) is made by KryoTale Studio (“we”). For any question about this policy, write to KryoTaleStudio@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

To remember where you are, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • the stops you unlocked, your stars and your diamonds,
  • the platform, train and background designs you unlocked in the shop,
  • your hint and undo counts,
  • your sound, music and ad preferences.

This data is never sent to us. We do not run servers and we do not use analytics. Settings → İlerlemeyi sıfırla (reset progress) in the app deletes your progress; deleting the app removes all of it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Kelime İstasyonu is free and supported by ads. Full-screen ads are shown between some stops; rewarded ads appear only when you choose them (for an extra hint, an extra undo or to double your diamond reward). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited). To show and measure ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version,
  • ad interaction data such as which ads were shown or tapped,
  • diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

Google collects and processes this data under its own policies; it does not reach us in a form that identifies you. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • iPhone: before your advertising identifier is used, the app asks for permission with Apple’s tracking prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: at first launch the app asks whether you allow personalized ads; you can change your answer in the app under Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve reklamlar. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Google → Ads.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google’s consent form is also shown. You can change your answer in the app under Ayarlar → Reklam tercihlerim (Ad choices).

The game is fully playable whatever you choose.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location. Music, sound and vibration are generated on your device and can be turned off in the app’s settings.

Children

Kelime İstasyonu is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences and parental guidance.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. To exercise your rights under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or the GDPR in the EEA / UK, or to raise a concern, write to the address above. For data collected by Google you can also use the tools on Google’s privacy page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
