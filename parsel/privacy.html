Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short: parsel has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob uses to show ads, and you control that with the choices described below.

Who we are

parsel (“the app”) is made by KryoTale Studio (“we”). If you have any question about this policy, write to KryoTaleStudio@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

To remember where you left off, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your level for each board size and any puzzle in progress,
  • your daily streak,
  • your settings (language, sound, haptics, dark mode),
  • counters that space out ads (for example, how many puzzles you solved since the last ad).

This data is never sent to us. We do not run any servers and we do not use analytics tools. You can erase your progress at any time with Settings → Reset Progress; deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

parsel is free and shows full-screen ads between puzzles, starting after your first few puzzles. The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC and, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version,
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped,
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information.

Google collects and processes this data under its own policies; we do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission with Apple’s tracking prompt before your advertising identifier can be used. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, it is not shared. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google’s consent form asks whether ads may be personalized. You can change your answer at any time in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location. Sound and vibration are produced on the device and can be switched off in the app’s settings.

Children

parsel is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences and parental guidance.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR) or in Türkiye (KVKK) and want to exercise your rights, or have any concern, contact us at the address above. For data collected by Google, you can also use the tools on Google’s privacy page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
