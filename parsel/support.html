How to play

Split the board into rectangles. Each number tells you how many squares its rectangle must cover, every rectangle must contain exactly one number, and no square may be left uncovered. Drag to draw a rectangle and tap one to remove it. The 💡 button shows where the next correct rectangle goes.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account or cloud sync, so deleting the app or switching to a new phone starts the game from the beginning.

How do I start over?
Open Settings → Reset Progress and tap the button twice.

Why do I see ads?
Ads keep parsel free. Your first few puzzles have no ads at all; after that an ad may appear between puzzles, at most once every few puzzles and never more than once every few minutes.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also use Settings → Ad Privacy inside the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
